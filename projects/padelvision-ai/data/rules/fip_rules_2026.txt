PADELVISION SOURCE MIRROR — FIP RULES EXCERPT

The complete official FIP Rules of Padel text is preserved in the original PadelVision v0.5 release archive. This GitHub source mirror contains only a lightweight excerpt so the local retrieval layer can start.

RULE 6. THE SERVICE OR SERVE
The serve must follow the official service-position, bounce, contact-height and diagonal-service-box requirements. Consult the official FIP Rules of Padel (application 01.01.2026) for complete wording and competition interpretation.

RULE 7. SERVICE FAULT
Service faults and second-service conditions are governed by the official FIP rulebook.

RULE 8. RETURN OF SERVE
The receiver must allow the serve to bounce in the service box before returning it, subject to the complete official rule wording.

RULE 13. POINT LOST
A player loses the point in situations defined by the official rules, including prohibited net contact and invalid returns.

IMPORTANT
This excerpt is not a replacement for the official FIP rulebook. For competition decisions, verify the current official FIP Rules of Padel.
