## Scientific / product honesty

- PadelVision's movement reference data is prototype coaching data and requires qualified coach validation before being treated as universal ground truth.
- VeriPath compatibility scores are decision-support scores, **not admission probabilities**.
- Synthetic programme records in VeriPath are discovery/demo data and must not be presented as verified university offerings.
